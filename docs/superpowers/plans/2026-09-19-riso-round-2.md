## Settled decisions

All ten were chosen by the user from explicit options.

### Inherited by all three prototypes

| decision | choice | why it was the choice |
|---|---|---|
| **Zoom model** | **Discrete plate change.** National and state are two separate renders with a transition. No free zoom. | A blow-up is a NEW plate, which is what riso does. Avoids MapLibre `CustomLayerInterface` + stencil-buffer risk entirely this round. |
| **Screen scale** | **Per-view plate.** National ~3.6px cell (fine, districts are tiny); state view re-screened to ~8px (coarse, dots become visibly dots). | The screen is a property of the plate. Directly fixes the legibility complaint and gains riso character at state level rather than losing it. Certainty cell ratios scale together so the encoding survives. |
| **Screen angles** | **Global, never per-district.** | Per-district angles are precisely what makes adjacent polygons vibrate — this is the "clash between districts" fix. Keylines stay the heaviest mark on the plate. |
| **Motion** | **Ambient registration breathing** (sub-pixel plate drift, slow cycle) **+ one-shot press cycle** on entering a state (plates lay down in sequence). | A real press never registers perfectly, so breathing is the most authentic motion riso has. **Screen rotation is ruled out**: creeping the halftone angle manufactures moiré, which the research paper correctly calls hostile to astigmatic and motion-sensitive readers. |
| **Reduced motion** | **Dot gain, no movement.** Breathing off entirely; press one-shot becomes a single non-spatial dot-gain pulse (halftone radius swells ~5% and settles). | The research paper's own suggestion. Keeps a sense of "something printed" without moving anything spatially. |
| **Senate layer** | **Replace at national, both at state.** National toggle swaps geometry (441 districts ↔ states). In a state blow-up, House districts stay as the fill and Senate money gets its own plate in the margin. | The units are not comparable at national scale. A state blow-up is the one place both scales genuinely belong on the same page, because they are the same state. |
| **Senate "banked" mark** | **Reuse the coarse screen.** One meaning across both layers: *this figure is not what it appears to be*. | Superseded boundary and banked-for-a-future-cycle are both that. One grammar to learn, stated plainly in each layer's legend. |
| **Cycle** | **2026 only.** | User's explicit choice, against the recommendation of 2024. See the mid-cycle caveat below — it is not optional. |

### What varies — the three prototypes

- **D — Three-Plate.** Money only, extended tonal range. Three plates:
  green → teal → federal blue → deep navy. Must stay **monotonic in OKLab L**;
  hue rides along as a secondary channel, which is what makes it a legitimate
  sequential ramp rather than a rainbow.
- **E — Tilt.** Bivariate. **Lightness = money, hue = REP/DEM tilt** of the
  recipient. **Diverging: two hues plus a neutral gray midpoint, never a hue at
  the midpoint.** 2026 House money is REP $172.8M / DEM $154.8M — near-even, so
  district-level variation is real rather than a one-sided wash.
- **F — Sector Plates.** Three *literal* plates — **Corporate, Trade
  Association, Labor** — each at its own screen angle, overprinted. A
  district's colour **is** its donor mix; total ink is total money. The most
  riso-true answer to "more colour depending on the size of the donations".

## Carried forward from round 1 — do not re-break these

Full detail in `web/prototypes/COMPARISON.md`.

1. **Validate categorical inks at `--pairs all`, not adjacent.** A choropleth is
   not a bar chart. The v1 plan's set fails this way (`#7E5BB0↔#2F4B9B` ΔE 13.4,
   under the 15 floor).
2. **Validate every ramp against its own paper.** The plan's ramp was checked
   against `#fcfcfb`; on warm riso stock its palest step reads 1.05:1.
3. **Chroma: lowest that passes, not highest that separates.** A ramp snapped
   for maximum separation lands on the gamut edge and reads as a dashboard.
4. **Kubelka-Munk has no dark mode.** Subtractive ink on a dark ground collapses
   to black. Dark mode composites **additively**; the dark inks are separately
   validated. This is a permanent two-model split.
5. **Cap halftone coverage at 0.88.** At 1.0 the dots close up, the cell
   vanishes, and the certainty encoding silently dies on the highest-money
   districts. Load-bearing, not taste.
6. **`export_data.py` rewinds rings for d3-geo**, which winds opposite to
   RFC 7946. Without it every district reads as the whole sphere minus itself
   (TX-21: `geoArea` 12.5660 sr, centroid in the Indian Ocean). Misleading
   symptom — outlines draw fine while every fill is the clip rectangle.
   **d3 only**; tippecanoe and MapLibre are planar and indifferent.
7. **AS/GU/MP/PR/VI cannot be drawn on Albers USA** and do not fail quietly —
   each returns the full clip rectangle and paints over the map, and they
   poison `fitExtent`. Probe with a *plain unfitted* projection, partition them
   out, render as chips, keep them in the table.
8. **`SECTOR_ORDER` is measured, not assumed** — top five by dollars. Party is
   13th, not 5th.
