Prototype A — Broadside

Thesis: registration quality encodes certainty — but on the keyline, not the fill. A superseded district's boundary doubles and shifts; its money stays exactly where it is. This answers the research paper's strongest objection (offset fills bleed into the neighbour and invent a polygon) without giving up the visual variable.

Cost: 441 SVG paths, three baked PNG plates (27 KB), zero per-frame work. Ports to MapLibre as ordinary fill and line layers plus a second line layer filtered on map_status with a line-translate — no custom layer, no shader, no stencil-buffer risk.

Ink: all five validated at --pairs all against this paper; ramp validated --ordinal. Dark stock is selected from its own validated set, never flipped.
