Prototype C — Specimen

Thesis: the subject is money, so the idiom is the one money is printed in. Tone is a line screen the way an engraver builds value, and certainty is the engraving convention: clean parallel ruling where the map governs, cross-hatching where it does not. Cross-hatch has meant shadow and doubt since long before anyone drew a congressional district.

Why it may be the right one: it is the only one of the three whose visual language is about the subject rather than about the printing process. It also gives the certainty encoding a second channel — line direction — which survives greyscale, print and colour-vision deficiency without relying on hue at all.

Why it may not be: banknote furniture is close to a tone this project may not want. A site about money in politics that dresses itself as currency risks looking like it is celebrating it.
