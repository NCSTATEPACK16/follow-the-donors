Three ways to print this map

All three show the same thing: $420.32M of federal PAC money, placed in 441 congressional districts, 2024 cycle. Real geometry, real money, real map_status — nothing here is a mockup.

They differ in one decision: what riso is for. The v1 plan says registration quality encodes certainty. The research paper argues that is optically unsound and proposes screen frequency instead. Neither of them considers the possibility that the right idiom is not the printing process at all but the subject. Each prototype takes one of those positions all the way down, so the choice can be made by looking rather than by arguing.

Every ink was produced by the dataviz validator against its own paper at --pairs all, not chosen off an ink chart. Two errors in the plan's recorded palette were found doing it — see COMPARISON.md.
