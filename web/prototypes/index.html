Three ways to colour this map

Round 1 asked what riso is for and settled it: the halftone plate won. Round 2 asks the next question — what the colour should mean — and adds the three things the plate could not do: a state blow-up, a Senate layer, and motion.

All three below show $328.79M of federal PAC money placed in 441 congressional districts, plus $80.87M to Senate candidates, 2026 cycle. Real geometry, real money, real map_status. 2026 is still being filed — every page carries that caveat as a band, not a footnote, because the figures are as filed and not final.

Every ink here was solved against the tone its plates actually print, not chosen off an ink chart and not checked as a swatch — round 1's ramps were typed by hand and the map was never what got validated. See COMPARISON.md for what building these turned up: a coverage cap applied to one end of the range and not the other, a legend that showed a different map, and the measured limit of a bivariate riso map.
